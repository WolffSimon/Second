# Question set template

Group by the requirement served. Number every question. Two sentences each. Say what "enough" looks like.

## [Requirement group, in the posting's own heading]

1. [Did you ever…] [name a client, artefact, number, system, role or decision]. [What "enough" looks like: "a name and a year is enough".]

## Reopened

Questions asked before and unanswered, restated once. If unanswered again, close the line.

## For the record

- What is confidential and stays out of every document?
- Who approves, and on what terms is the work being done?
- What fact, if true, would mean this whole thing is wrong?

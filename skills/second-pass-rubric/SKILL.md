# Second pass rubric

A first build is a draft until this has run.

## (a) Identify what the document does not answer

Take the posting line by line. For each requirement, find the sentence in the document that answers it with an instance: a thing done, with a number or a name. A sentence that answers with an adjective, a habit-claim or the posting's own words does not count. List the unanswered lines.

## (b) Search the inventory

For each unanswered line, search the whole inventory, not the part that seems relevant. Receipts sit under the era they happened in, and the relevant one is often filed under a different function. Place every receipt found, in the cell where it happened, at the altitude of the seat. Re-check numbers once after placing.

## (c) Ask for what remains

Run `excavation-interview` on the lines still unanswered. Apply the answers. Bank them with `receipts-inventory`. A "no" closes the line: the document stays silent there by the invented-gap rule.

## The embarrassment test

Read every sentence as the person, across a table from the reader, and ask: would I be embarrassed to defend this? It catches what the other passes miss: a day's work reported as an achievement; the reader's own vocabulary borrowed for the person's organisation; a heroic verb where a plain one is true; a volunteer credential standing in a profile; a number told twice; a claim the profile makes larger than the body supports; the device from a banned family that returned under momentum.

## Language pass

Every paragraph opener checked for the fronted noun phrase and the identity announcement. Every sentence checked for absolutes, balanced antitheses and metaphor where a literal phrase exists. Every compound sentence with more than one subject split. Every echo of the posting rewritten.

## Numbers once, again

Placing receipts moves numbers. Re-sweep the document for any figure appearing twice, and for any figure appearing in a capability block.

## Report

Tell the person what was placed, from where, and what remains as questions. Then the document is finished, or it waits on answers.

# The receipts inventory

The inventory is canon. Documents are built from it, never the other way round. If a document contains a fact the inventory does not, the document is wrong until the fact is banked or removed.

## Structure

- **Consistency rules at the head.** Numbered rules that resolve known ambiguities: which era a fact belongs to; which two facts must never be conflated; which number is the canonical form of a figure that has been stated several ways; which facts are stated plainly and which are provenance only.
- **Headline numbers, once each,** with their canonical wording and the one place each belongs by default (profile or cell).
- **Receipts by era,** in the person's own words where possible, dated on entry.
- **Addenda,** each headed with the date, the source (which conversation or voice note) and the person's words, appended rather than merged so provenance survives.
- **Usage rulings,** especially for credentials: use, situational, never, with the reason. Rulings are per reader, and a ruling can be reversed by the person for one document without changing the rule.
- **Open questions,** listing what has been asked and not answered, so nothing is asked twice and nothing is assumed.

## Rules of entry

- Bank the day it is disclosed. A fact left in a conversation is lost at the next compaction.
- Bank in the person's words, then the document form if it differs ("CIEC" in speech; "the parent's India engineering centre" in documents).
- Bank the restriction with the fact: "true, never used", "conversation only", "name in inventory only".
- Bank corrections as corrections, dated, with the wrong form marked withdrawn, so a fork cannot resurrect it.
- Never invent a receipt. Never estimate a number the person has not given. If a figure is "estimated," bank the estimation method beside it.

## Checking a document against canon

Every number in the document exists in the inventory in that form. Every claim's relation is the one the inventory records (organisation did versus person did). Every credential on the page has a ruling that permits it for this reader. Every name on the page is permitted by its handling note. Anything that fails is fixed in the document, not in the inventory.
